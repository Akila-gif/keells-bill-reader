Keells Bill Reader

Reads your Nexus e-bills straight from the screen

On-device · nothing uploaded

Watch the screen

  1. Show the bill on this computer: mirror your phone (for example with 3uAirPlayer or AirPlay), or open the e-bill here.
  2. Press Start screen monitor and choose the window that shows the bill.
  3. Open the bill in the Keells Nexus app and scroll slowly to the end, pausing on each screenful.

Read a bill from a screen recording

Phones don't let a website watch other apps, so record the screen while you scroll the bill, then open the recording here. It is read on this phone.

    Screenshots work too: take one for each part of the bill and choose them all.

    The bill is found automatically. If the window shows other things too, drag on the picture to mark where the bill is. Clear the marked area
